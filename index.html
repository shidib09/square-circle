01 / Our club

A local tradition.
A lasting connection.

Square Circle Sportsmen of Camden County is a sporting club in Gibbsboro, New Jersey, with roots dating to 1939.

Fishing, hunting, and the shooting sports are part of its sporting identity. At its heart is something simple: people connecting through a shared interest in the outdoors.

A few things to know

Can I visit without a membership?

Please contact the club before visiting. Guest arrangements and access requirements should be confirmed directly with the club.

How do I apply for membership?

Call (856) 435-9722 to ask about current openings, sponsorship requirements, application steps, and dues.

Where can I find event information?

Check the club’s Facebook listing and CMP event listing. Confirm event details with the club before attending.

What should I know before using the range?

Confirm authorization and complete any required orientation first. Follow posted club rules and all range officer instructions.
